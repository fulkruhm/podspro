## Slide 2: The Problem

### Supply chains still run too reactively

- inventory issues are caught after service levels drop
- planning teams lose time stitching together reports
- forecasting is disconnected from live workflows
- leaders lack a clear audit trail of decisions and actions

- Focus on operational pain, not technical pain.
- Make the cost of delay and fragmentation explicit.
